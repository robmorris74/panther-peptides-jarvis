Incoming QC SOP - Peptide lots (RUO resale)

Scope
- Applies to all incoming peptide lots received for resale.

Procedure
1. Receiving
- Record arrival date, carrier tracking, supplier packing slip, and internal PO number.
- Visual inspection of external packaging for damage and temperature indicator if shipped cold.

2. Documentation check
- Verify COA present and matches packing slip (lot number, peptide sequence, quantity, purity).
- Verify MSDS/SDS if applicable.

3. Quarantine
- Place in quarantine area and assign internal inventory ID. Do not release for sale until QC acceptance.

4. Sampling & testing (spot-check)
- For high-volume or flagship products, select random samples for independent HPLC and MS testing.
- Document method used and acceptance criteria (e.g., purity within ±3% of COA for research-grade).

5. Storage
- Store per supplier recommendations (ambient, 2–8°C, -20°C) in monitored storage with temperature logs.

6. Release
- If COA matches and no visual or documentation issues, label and move to available stock.
- If discrepancies found, follow nonconformance procedure.

7. Nonconformance & return
- Tag lot as 'hold' and notify supplier with nonconformance report containing photos, COA, and discrepancy description.
- If supplier accepts, arrange replacement or credit; otherwise escalate to legal/commercial.

8. Recordkeeping
- Retain COA, shipping records, photos, testing records and internal QC form for each lot.

Acceptance criteria
- COA present and matches, no damage to packaging, storage conditions maintained, spot-check testing within acceptance.

Safety
- Wear PPE when handling powders; follow MSDS guidance for solvents/compounds.
