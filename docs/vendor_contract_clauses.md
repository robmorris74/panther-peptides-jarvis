Essential vendor contract clauses for peptide sourcing (resale, RUO)

1. Definitions & scope
- Define RUO products and exclude human/veterinary claims.

2. Documentation & COA
- Supplier must provide COA for every lot; COA must include HPLC and MS data and impurity profile.

3. Lot traceability & labeling
- Supplier must provide lot number, production date, expiry, storage conditions and recommended labeling language (RUO).

4. Warranties & indemnification
- Warranty for conformity to COA at time of shipment.
- Indemnification for breaches including mislabeling, contamination.

5. Right-to-audit & inspections
- Right to audit manufacturing records with reasonable notice.

6. Returns & non-conforming material
- Procedures for quarantine, replacement, and refund for off-spec lots.

7. Export controls & regulatory compliance
- Supplier represents compliance with export controls and restricted substance lists; must notify of any export restrictions.

8. Confidentiality & IP
- NDA clauses for custom sequences and IP handling.

9. Pricing & MOQ
- Pricing structure, discounts, MOQ, lead times, and termination clauses.

10. Termination & liability limits
- Termination for breach; caps on liability consistent with company policy but ensure carve-outs for willful misconduct.

11. Force majeure & change control
- Notice of changes in manufacturing site or methods and right to terminate.

12. Governing law & dispute resolution
- Specify jurisdiction and arbitration where desired.
