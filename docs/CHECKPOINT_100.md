# Jarvis Checkpoint 100

Checkpoint 100 consolidates the Panther Peptides Jarvis operating agent through six production-control stages added after v70.

## v75 — Service assurance
- Released-inventory reservation controls
- Lot expiry monitoring
- Customer support SLA checks
- Supplier review scheduling
- Consolidated service-assurance score

## v80 — Governance v2
- Hash-stamped research-only policy snapshots
- Lot release dossiers
- Business-document validation
- Zero-default risk budgets
- Governance scoring

## v85 — Business planning
- Demand forecasting from order history
- Inventory-aging risk
- Cash-runway snapshots
- SKU concentration analysis
- Side-effect-free planning cycle

## v90 — Continuity
- Critical dependency inventory
- Backup existence/integrity verification
- Tabletop recovery drills
- Continuity plans
- Continuity score

## v95 — Security posture
- Internal security event journal
- Secret-rotation reviews
- Owner access reviews
- Hash-stamped audit checkpoints
- Security posture score

## v100 — Governor
- Explicit autonomy contracts
- Go-live certification
- Operational maturity scoring
- Self-evaluation of hard guardrails
- Owner brief snapshots
- Bounded internal governor loop

## Non-negotiable controls
Jarvis keeps product publication, procurement, inventory release, consequential refunds, paid shipping-label purchase, and marketing behind owner approval. All checkpoint 100 governor/assurance cycles are designed to be side-effect free unless a separately authorized connector action is invoked through its existing approval path.

Panther Peptides customer-facing positioning remains: **FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY — NOT FOR HUMAN OR VETERINARY USE.**
