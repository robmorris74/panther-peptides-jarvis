Purpose
- Standard checklist to vet peptide suppliers for a resale RUO business.

Must-have documentation
- Certificate of Analysis (COA) for each lot: HPLC chromatogram, MS spectrum, reported purity, water content, residual solvents.
- Manufacturing site information and contact person.
- Batch/lot traceability (lot numbers, production date, expiry).
- Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) or Safety Data Sheet (SDS) where applicable.

Quality & manufacturing
- Manufacturing standard: GMP, ISO, or research-grade. Document any claims.
- Peptide synthesis method if provided (SPPS, recombinant) and typical scale.
- Purification methods and typical purity ranges for offered grades.
- Analytical methods used for COA (HPLC method, column, solvent, MS instrument).

Regulatory & restrictions
- Confirmation supplier will label as FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY (RUO) if contract requires.
- Disallowed / restricted product confirmation: supplier must disclose if they supply regulated peptides (growth factors, hormones, toxins) and any export restrictions.
- Export control and ITAR/EAR compliance statement where applicable.

Logistics & packaging
- Typical lead times and MOQ (minimum order quantity).
- Packaging for stability (amber vials, desiccants, foil, cold-chain options).
- Shipping partners and cold-chain capabilities (dry ice, insulated shippers).

Traceability & quality response
- Recall and nonconformance procedures.
- Right-to-audit clause availability.
- Warranty and indemnification terms for off-spec material.

Commercial
- Pricing tiers and volume discounts.
- Payment terms, credit terms, returns policy.
- Confidentiality and IP handling for custom sequences or proprietary work.

Red flags (fail and escalate)
- No COA or incomplete COA.
- Refusal to provide lot traceability or site information.
- Vague responses about controlled peptides or export restrictions.
- No corrective action process or refusal of right-to-audit.

Recommended acceptance criteria
- COA present and matches our independent spot-check (where performed).
- Clear packaging and lot traceability, lead times acceptable to business model.
- Contractual agreement on RUO labeling and returns/replacements.
